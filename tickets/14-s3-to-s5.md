## What it makes work

After the user picks L0 or L1, `/diy-harness` builds the harness with them:

- S3 designs the smallest set of parts that meets the goal, each with what it answers and its proof, and parks
  the rest. At L0 the spec is one section of `STATE.md`.
- S4 cuts the spec into tracer-bullet tickets. At L0 it is skipped: S3 writes the one ticket.
- S5 builds one ticket per pass: proof red, part built, proof green, review, commit on the user's yes. At L1 a
  guard is a script with a must-block and a must-allow test (`docs/adr/0003` G1 to G6).
- The M2 rulings are in the text: `status` invites no answer, `LEVELS.md` names the mixed tier and a sandbox
  already on, S1 recommends tier B (D14). A resume at S3 to S5 carries on with the stage.
